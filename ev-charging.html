EV charge point installation

Home and workplace electric vehicle charging, installed by an OZEV registered electrician.

An EV charge point is a permanent addition to your electrical installation, not an accessory. It needs the right position, the right protection and a supply that can actually take it. Sean is OZEV registered, which means he is on the government's approved installer list for electric vehicle charge points.

Advice first, installation second

Before anything is fitted, Sean will look at where you park, what your incoming supply can support, and how the cable can be run neatly. It is a short conversation that avoids the two things people regret most: a charger in an awkward spot, and cable run where it should not be.

Home charging

For most households a charge point on the drive or garage wall is straightforward, but the detail matters: the position relative to where the car sits, the route back to the consumer unit, the protective devices required, and whether the existing board can take another circuit. Sean will go through all of it with you before quoting.

Workplace and commercial charging

Workplaces install charge points for staff, fleet vehicles or visitors. The questions are the same but bigger: how many units, where they sit relative to the supply, and what the site can support without an upgrade. Sean can advise on what is realistic before you commit to a plan.

How the job runs

  1. Talk it throughTell Sean about your parking, your vehicle and where you would like the unit.
  2. Check the supplyThe existing installation is assessed to confirm what it can take.
  3. Agree position and priceYou see the proposed position and route, and get a clear cost.
  4. Install, test, certifyFitted neatly, tested, and handed over with the paperwork and a walk-through of how to use it.

Grants, eligibility criteria and charger brands change regularly. Rather than publish claims that may be out of date, ask Sean what currently applies to your situation and he will give you the up to date position.

EV charging questions

What does OZEV registered mean?

OZEV is the Office for Zero Emission Vehicles. Being OZEV registered means Sean is on the government's approved installer list for electric vehicle charge points.

Where can the charge point go?

That depends on your parking, your incoming supply and the run of cable required. Sean will look at the options with you before anything is fitted, and will explain the trade-offs between the tidiest position and the most practical one.

Can you fit charge points at a workplace?

Yes. Workplace and commercial installations are covered as well as domestic ones, including multiple units where the supply allows.

Which charger should I choose?

Ask Sean directly. Suitability depends on your vehicle, your supply and how you will use it, so it is better answered for your specific situation than promised in advance on a web page.

Thinking about an EV charge point?

Send Sean a photo of where you park and where your consumer unit is, and he can tell you a lot from that alone.
