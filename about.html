About Sean Warrender

26 years in the trade, based in Shrewsbury, working across Shropshire and the surrounding counties.

One electrician, start to finish

SW Electrical is Sean Warrender. He has been an electrician for 26 years, working across domestic and commercial jobs, and he still does the work himself. When you ring, you speak to the person who will be doing the job, and when he quotes it, he is the one who has to stand behind it.

That is the main practical difference between a business this size and a larger contractor. Nothing gets lost between the person who priced it and the person who turns up, and there is no incentive to sell you work you do not need.

How Sean works

The approach is simple and has not changed much in 26 years. Look at the job properly. Explain what is going on in language that makes sense. Say what needs doing now and what can wait. Do the work safely, test it, certify it, and leave the place tidy.

Sometimes that means telling someone their installation is fine and they do not need to spend anything. That is part of the job too.

Registrations

SW Electrical is registered with NAPIT for electrical work, and Sean is OZEV registered for EV charge point installation. Air conditioning is planned for the future and will be offered only once the relevant registrations are confirmed.

Local, and staying that way

Sean is based in Shrewsbury and most work is within Shropshire, with parts of Staffordshire, the Midlands and Cheshire also covered. Working locally is deliberate. It keeps travel time down, it means he can get to an emergency, and in a county this size, reputation still travels by word of mouth.
