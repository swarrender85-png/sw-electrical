Air conditioning, coming soon

Air conditioning installation and servicing is planned for the future. It is not available yet.

COMING SOON

What is happening

SW Electrical intends to add air conditioning installation and servicing to the services on offer. Air conditioning work requires its own registrations, separate from electrical registration, and Sean will not take on that work until those are in place.

To be clear about certification

SW Electrical is not currently REFCOM or F-Gas registered. Those registrations are expected before any air conditioning work is offered. Until this page says otherwise, no air conditioning work is being quoted, booked or carried out.

Register your interest

If air conditioning is something you are planning for a home or a business, it is worth flagging now. Sean will keep a note of who has asked and get in touch once the service goes live, rather than you having to keep checking back.

Use the contact form and mention air conditioning in the description. Include the property type and roughly what you are hoping to cool, and Sean will be able to have a useful conversation with you when the time comes.

In the meantime

Air conditioning installations still need a suitable electrical supply, and that part is very much live today. If you are planning ahead for a room, an extension or a commercial space, Sean can look at the domestic or commercial electrical side now so you are ready later.

Air conditioning questions

Can I book air conditioning work now?

No. Air conditioning is planned but not yet offered. Nothing is being booked until the required registrations are confirmed.

Is SW Electrical REFCOM or F-Gas registered?

Not currently. Those registrations are expected before any air conditioning work is offered, and this page will be updated when that is confirmed.

What can I do in the meantime?

Register your interest using the contact form and mention air conditioning. Sean will get in touch once the service is live.
